Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about finding comedy shows, checking listings, buying tickets and submitting events to UK Comedy Map.

UK Comedy Map FAQs

Select a question to reveal the answer, or search below.

What is UK Comedy Map?

UK Comedy Map is a free website that helps you discover stand-up comedy shows, comedy clubs, open mic nights and live comedy events across the UK. You can browse by city, region, venue or event.

Is UK Comedy Map free to use?

Yes. UK Comedy Map is free to browse and does not require a subscription or account.

How do I find comedy shows near me?

Use the search and filter tools on the homepage, browse one of the city pages, or choose your area from the Regions menu. Each listing includes the available date, venue and ticket information.

Which cities does UK Comedy Map cover?

UK Comedy Map features listings throughout the UK, with dedicated pages for London, Manchester, Leeds, Birmingham and Bristol, alongside wider regional pages covering England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.

What types of comedy events are listed?

Listings may include stand-up comedy, mixed-bill club nights, open mic nights, work-in-progress shows, touring comedians, comedy festivals and other live comedy events.

How often are the comedy listings updated?

Listings are refreshed regularly from venue websites and trusted event sources. Shows can still be changed, postponed or cancelled at short notice, so always confirm the final details on the official ticket or venue page.

Can I buy tickets through UK Comedy Map?

UK Comedy Map does not normally sell tickets directly. Show listings link to the venue, promoter or ticket provider so you can check availability and complete your booking there.

Are show times, line-ups and prices guaranteed?

No. We work to keep listings useful and accurate, but times, prices, venues and line-ups can change. Please check the official event page before travelling or purchasing tickets.

Can I search for a particular comedian or venue?

Yes. Use the site search to enter the name of a comedian, comedy club, venue, city or event. Results depend on the information available in the current listings.

Do you list open mic comedy nights?

Yes. Open mic and lower-cost comedy nights are included when suitable listings are available. You can also use the Cheap / Open Mic filter on relevant listings pages.

Does UK Comedy Map cover comedy festivals?

Yes. The site includes festival shows and guides when information is available, including coverage of major UK comedy festivals and fringe events.

How can a venue or promoter add an event?

Venue owners, comedy promoters and event organisers can contact UK Comedy Map with the event name, date, venue, location, ticket link and any suitable images. Submitted events are reviewed before inclusion.

What should I do if an event is missing?

Send the event details through the contact form below. Include the official event or ticket link so the listing can be checked.

How do I report incorrect information?

Use the contact form below and include the event name, venue, date and the detail that needs correcting. A link to the official source is helpful.

Can I use UK Comedy Map on my phone?

Yes. UK Comedy Map is designed to work on mobile phones, tablets, laptops and desktop computers.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can browse comedy listings, city pages, regional pages and the comedy map without creating an account.

How does UK Comedy Map collect event information?

Listings are gathered from publicly available venue information, promoter pages and event sources, then organised to make comedy events easier to discover in one place.

Why is a show no longer listed?

A listing may disappear because the event has passed, sold out, been cancelled, changed source, or no longer meets the site's listing criteria.
