Weekend comedy FAQs

Which dates count as this weekend?

The listings cover the next Friday, Saturday and Sunday. If you're visiting on Saturday or Sunday, they'll show the following weekend instead.

Can I find free or cheap comedy shows?

Yes, some clubs and open mic nights offer free or lower-priced entry. Check each listing for prices and booking details.

How do I book tickets?

Choose a show and select “View tickets” to visit the venue, organiser or ticket provider. That's where you can confirm prices and availability.

What if there aren't any shows near me?

Not every gig appears in the feed. Try the main search or browse your local comedy guides for more ideas.
