Free Comedy in London

Donation buckets, pub rooms and some of London’s best-value live comedy.

London has one of the busiest comedy scenes in the world, and you don't need to spend a fortune to see it. Between donation based nights, no cover charge pub gigs and clubs that have made free entry their entire identity, there's a genuinely brilliant night of stand-up on offer most days of the week if you know where to look. Here's where to find the best free comedy in London.

Angel Comedy Club

The undisputed heavyweight of London's free comedy scene. Angel Comedy runs completely free stand-up shows seven nights a week across two venues in Islington: The Bill Murray, its own permanent pub and comedy space on Queen's Head Street, and The Camden Head, the pub where it all began, on Camden Passage. Between the two venues you can catch anywhere from three to six consecutive shows a night, ranging from raw new material nights early in the week to more polished Friday and Saturday bills, with regular surprise drop ins from major names testing new hours. It's genuinely one of the best value nights out in the whole city, and the only real cost is buying a drink at the bar.

📍 The Bill Murray, 39 Queen's Head Street, Islington N1 8NQ 📍 The Camden Head, 2 Camden Walk, Islington N1 8DY

Rye Lane Ruckus

Quite possibly the best smelling comedy club in London, Rye Lane Ruckus runs out of Eagle Eats Bakery in Peckham and mixes established circuit acts with emerging talent in a genuinely unusual setting. Entry is free, though you'll probably want to pick up something from the bakery counter while you're there.

📍 Eagle Eats Bakery, Peckham

Downstairs at the King's Head

An unassuming basement venue in Muswell Hill that's built a loyal following over the years. Its Thursday Try Out Night gives newer acts a chance to test material for free, while Saturdays step things up with more established names for a small door charge.

📍 The King's Head, Muswell Hill

The Comedy Industrial Complex

Held at Pub on the Park in Hackney, this free night leans more towards topical and satirical comedy than straight stand-up, mixing in storytellers and character acts alongside more conventional sets. A good shout if you want something a little different from the standard club format.

📍 Pub on the Park, Hackney

Quintessentially Comedy

A friendly new act and material night at Quinn's in Camden, running every other Thursday and hosted by a rotating cast of local comperes. Free to attend and a good spot to catch comedians early in their careers before they start charging for tickets elsewhere.

📍 Quinn's, Camden

Tips for making the most of free comedy nights

Free doesn't mean turn up whenever you like. Rooms at Angel Comedy and similar clubs fill up fast, especially at weekends, so get there early if you want a seat rather than standing at the back. It's also worth buying a drink or two once you're in, since these venues rely on bar takings rather than ticket sales to keep the lights on and the comedians paid. And if a night is donation based, throwing a few pounds in the bucket at the end genuinely makes a difference to whether it keeps running.
