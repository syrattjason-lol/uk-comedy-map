Open Mic Comedy in Manchester

The spots to watch new material — or take your first five minutes on stage.

Manchester has quietly become one of the best cities in the UK to try your hand at stand-up, or just watch someone else do it for the first time. The city's open mic scene is friendly, well established and genuinely varied, running everything from full blown competitions to laid back pub nights where the bar is your only obligation. Here's where to find the best open mic comedy in Manchester.

Beat the Frog at The Frog & Bucket

The city's most famous new act night, held every Monday at the Frog & Bucket in the Northern Quarter. Amateur comedians get five minutes to win the crowd over before facing the dreaded "croak off," where the audience decides whether they've earned the right to keep going. It's a proper baptism of fire, and it's launched genuine careers, with past winners including Jason Manford and John Bishop.

📍 102 Oldham Street, Manchester M4 1LJ

Comedy Balloon at Ape & Apple

A well loved Wednesday night that mixes an opening set of established comedians with a middle section of new and newish acts, before rounding off with more of a headline feel. The room is upstairs, properly set up with a real stage and decent sound, and it's worth emailing ahead if you want to be considered for a spot, though it's not guaranteed.

📍 Ape & Apple, 28-30 John Dalton Street, Manchester M2 6HQ

Easy Comedy at Creatures

Creatures Comedy Club's Tuesday night offering, a cheap and cheerful evening built around new material and up and coming acts, with the occasional established comedian dropping in to try out fresh material of their own. At just a few pounds on the door, it's one of the best value comedy nights in the city.

📍 Creatures Comedy Club, 42-44 Oldham Street, Manchester M4 1LE

The Laughienda

Technically a mixed bill rather than a pure open mic, but The Laughienda deserves a mention here for how much stage time it gives to newer acts. Held weekly at The Lion's Den on Deansgate, it's entirely donation funded and mixes complete beginners with more experienced comedians and the occasional TV name, all on the same free bill.

📍 The Lion's Den, Deansgate Mews, Manchester M3 4EN

Comedy Open Mic at The Fitzgerald

A relaxed, no pressure night in the Northern Quarter where anyone can put their name down and get up on stage, whether you're a seasoned circuit act testing new bits or a total first timer. Free entry, and a genuinely supportive room for anyone nervous about their first ever set.

📍 The Fitzgerald, 11 Stevenson Square, Northern Quarter, Manchester M1 1DB

The Comedy Circus at Stage and Radio

A newer addition to the Manchester open mic scene, mixing comedians of varying experience levels in a laid back bar setting. Worth checking listings for exact dates as the schedule tends to shift month to month.

📍 Stage and Radio, Manchester

Tips for your first open mic set

If you're thinking about getting up yourself rather than just watching, message the organiser ahead of time wherever possible, as most nights work on a sign up list and spots fill quickly. Keep your first set short and simple rather than trying to cram in everything you've got, and remember that every comedian on that bill was once exactly where you are now. Manchester crowds are known for being warm and encouraging, especially at nights like Beat the Frog and The Laughienda, so there's genuinely no better city to give it a go.
