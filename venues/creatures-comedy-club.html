Creatures Comedy Club Manchester

Creatures Comedy Club is an independent basement comedy venue in Manchester’s Northern Quarter. It runs stand-up comedy seven nights a week, including hour-long early shows, weeknight showcases, weekend line-ups and touring performances.

Address

42–44 Oldham Street
Manchester
M4 1LE

Regular show times

Pay-what-you-think shows run Monday to Friday at 7:00pm. Later showcases commonly start at 8:30pm. Sunday shows are listed at 5:30pm and 8:30pm.

Doors

Doors normally open 30 minutes before the advertised show time.

Tickets and seating

Tickets can be shown on a phone. Seating is first come, first served, and groups are seated together where possible.

Official website Instagram @creaturescomedyclub Open in Google Maps
Accessibility: Creatures is a basement venue accessed by stairs and currently does not have step-free or wheelchair access. Contact the venue before booking if you have specific access requirements.

Comedy every night

The venue advertises comedy seven nights a week. Monday-to-Friday early shows are commonly pay-what-you-think, with a small amount paid to secure a ticket and optional contributions after the performance. Later evening showcases feature mixed line-ups, while weekends include larger Friday and Saturday showcases.

What to expect

Creatures hosts local acts, comedians from across the UK and international performers. Its programme includes new material, game shows, themed comedy nights, previews, tour shows and festival performances.
